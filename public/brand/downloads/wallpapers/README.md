# Experimental Software wallpapers and banners

White Room 1.1 — https://experimental.software/brand#wallpapers

The Mac wallpapers use Muted gray, with white corner branding and an acid SOFTWARE LAB flag. iPhone wallpapers and profile banners come in paper (light) and ink (dark). Each includes a ready-to-use PNG and an SVG with outlined lettering.

- iPhone Lock Screen: 1320 × 2868. Keep wallpaper zoom at minimum. The clock and bottom controls have clear space; other iPhone sizes may crop slightly.
- iPhone Home Screen: 1320 × 2868. A blank app area with the Outlier mark left of Search and the wordmark to its right. Both align to Search's vertical center, and each is horizontally centered between Search and its screen edge. In Wallpaper settings, customize the Home Screen separately, choose this photo, turn Blur off, and avoid extra zooming. Placement is calibrated to the supplied iPhone screenshot with Search above the dock; other layouts may need adjustment.
- macOS / 16:9 monitors: 5120 × 2880. Muted gray with no lines, a white logo and wordmark at bottom left, and the acid SOFTWARE LAB flag at bottom right.
- MacBook Air (13-inch) / 20:13: 2560 × 1664, matching the built-in display including the menu bar area. The original vector artwork has equal 48-pixel insets from the sides and bottom.
- Choose the Mac wallpaper that matches each display's aspect ratio and use Fill Screen. Fit to Screen preserves artwork on other ratios but changes its distance from the screen edges. macOS can use a separate wallpaper on each connected display. The original centered paper/ink Mac wallpapers (3840 × 2400, 16:10) are retained in this kit.
- LinkedIn: 1584 × 396. For personal profiles, not company pages.
- X / Twitter: 1500 × 500. The layout gives the desktop profile photo room and keeps branding clear of the mobile status bar and app controls. Keep the image centered when uploading.

Choose a PNG in your device's wallpaper settings or profile image uploader. Profile crops vary by viewport.

The artwork preserves the original Outlier mark, General Sans and Meslo LG S lettering, and White Room colors. SVGs have no external font dependencies. Font binaries are not included.
