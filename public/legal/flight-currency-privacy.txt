Flight Currency for Google Flights — Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 16, 2026

Flight Currency (previously Google Flights Currency Setter) is provided by
Experimental Software LLC.

The extension saves one preferred currency code using Chrome's built-in
storage.sync API. Chrome may synchronize this preference between browsers
signed into the same account when browser sync is enabled.

The extension checks Google Flights URLs and changes their currency parameter
on your device. It does not send flight searches, browsing history, personal
information, or your chosen currency to Experimental Software or any external
service. There are no analytics, ads, accounts, remote code, or servers operated
for this extension.

The content script is limited to Google Travel pages so it can detect navigation
into Google Flights. It changes only Google Flights URLs. The storage permission
saves the selected currency. Your preference remains in Chrome storage until
changed or removed through Chrome's extension or browser-data controls.

Google Flights and Chrome are subject to Google's own privacy policies. Airlines
and booking sites operate independently. Flight Currency is not affiliated with
or endorsed by Google.

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will appear at this URL.
